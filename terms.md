Hospital, emergency-department, trauma-center and heliport information comes from **public sources**: the U.S. Homeland Infrastructure Foundation-Level Data (HIFLD), the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), and OpenStreetMap contributors.

- That information **can be incomplete, outdated or wrong**. Hospitals close, lose or change trauma designations, divert ambulances and change phone numbers. Heliports change status.
- Drive times and routes come from Apple Maps and are estimates. Traffic, road closures and conditions change.
- Weather comes from Apple Weather and aviationweather.gov and may not reflect conditions where you are.
- Candidate landing zones found through OpenStreetMap are unverified outlines on a map, not inspected sites.
- Field notes and team LZs are entered by you or your team. I do not check them.

**Always verify critical information**, for example by calling the hospital, confirming with dispatch, or inspecting the site, whenever time allows. You use all information in the app at your own risk.
